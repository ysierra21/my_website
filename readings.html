Response #1

Photographs are essentially a form of capturing a moment in time. It allows individuals to recall the past for themselves or even show off their experiences to others. The act of capturing images allows for people to reveal their personal values and interests. It is somewhat similar to creating graphics, in the sense that the process is intentional. Both art forms typically require an individual to have the desire to specifically document something in a certain way. The way in which a person chooses to take a photograph matters. It shows how they individually observe the world and it shows what it is that they would like to share. This could be demonstrated through the lighting, angles, focus point, and framing. When taking photographs, individuals are effectively offering a part of themselves. It is a glimpse of their unique perspectives and standards for what they are choosing to document. While it may be argued that photographs are merely a reflection of reality, there is still room for interpretation and bias such as in other forms of art. Therefore, photographs should not be interpreted as an absolute reflection of reality but rather a part of a story from one individual's perspective.

Capturing images includes but is not limited to documenting an activity, an object, a person, or a place. The art of photography has become increasingly relevant due to major advancements in technology and the dominance of social media. Photography has become more accessible than ever because of the simplicity in being able to participate. In the present day, it is easy for diverse groups of people to use a phone to capture images and even use free mobile applications to edit them rather than needing to seek out expensive specialized equipment. Although that is still an option, it is usually reserved for professionals or people who are more serious about what they are hoping to capture and the way that they would like to display it. For many people, they may enjoy capturing everyday moments and sharing their life through an online presence. Even if they choose to keep those moments to themselves, most will not find the need for their photographs to be at a professional level. When individuals from the general population express that they want a moment to appear in the best possible way, it is typically because they genuinely find value in it. Under these circumstances, it is likely that they will seek out ways for their photographs to be of higher quality and be portrayed in the best possible light.

Moreover the ability to instantly share the average images that we capture, reinforces how we demonstrate the current notion of the world. Firstly, the act of placing those images into the public emphasizes the human need for connection as well as validation. There is a satisfaction that comes with sharing those moments. In a more general context, the singular use of photography shows the trends and aspects of cultures in our society. For instance, the subjects of the photographs may depict current fashion. Using that information, future generations can derive more details about the economy, the political climate, gender norms, and beauty standards.

Response #2

The daily use of artificial intelligence has slowly become normalized and is continuously increasing amongst individuals of all ages. The overall appeal comes from the various forms of aid that the tool can provide. For instance, these programs are able to complete tasks in a rapid manner and it can enhance the understanding of complex topics. It’s easy to become overly reliant on models such as Chat GPT because of its accessibility and functionality. In addition, artificial intelligence is largely used by large corporations and is a prominent part of most applications we use today. This might contribute to its harmless and rather friendly appearance. However, this rising dependency can quickly become detrimental to individuals if it is not managed appropriately. The use of artificial intelligence affects the critical thinking skills and creativity that a person possesses. Studies suggest that the scientific explanation behind this may be the decrease in cognitive load. More simply put, the brain has to put in less effort to achieve the result that it wants since it can depend on artificial intelligence to do the heavy load. Given that Chat GPT is able to generate extensive results, many people take advantage of this whether they do it consciously or not. This essentially leads to long periods where essential parts of the brain are not being regularly activated in the ways it should. Many individuals use the tool to entirely perform tasks for them as opposed to working with the model to develop their own skills by inputting their own ideas.

In addition to the intellectual effects of relying on artificial intelligence, there are also major emotional aspects that come into play. A specific example is the use of virtual companions. When individuals turn to these models, it is because they recognize the need for support and companionship or even emotional intimacy. The issue lies with the idea that they are forming a genuine beneficial connection. Artificial intelligence is essentially trained to give answers to questions in a certain way and it responds to the individual only in specific ways. This would suggest that there is no way to realistically create a bond with the intelligence models. Being powered through the use of artificial intelligence they may be able to imitate the communication in a real relationship or create the illusion of having another person present but at the same time, the use of a virtual companion is not a substitute for living individuals because by nature they do not have their own substance to contribute. These models are manmade and it is necessary to acknowledge who is in control of programming these models and what is their long-term goal. The use of artificial intelligence for emotional dependence and stimulation also takes away from the physical significance of having other individuals around. Ironically, while it is able to generate responses and complete tasks efficiently, it becomes time consuming. This takes away from the opportunity for genuine contact, new experiences, and personal development in various areas of life.

Response #3

The story that I decided to focus on was titled Kadiatu: The grieving mother ‘The devil at my door’. By combining different forms of story telling, specifically the use of text, illustrations, photography, and video, the viewer is gently guided through a complex and emotional storyline. This story in particular depicted themes of grief, loss, and medical mistrust. By providing visual context, viewers are often able to absorb a story in a shorter amount of time and in some cases they will be able to understand it through a deeper lens. For instance, the same story could be depicted solely through words but it would be much longer. Illustrations are helpful to add because they represent what text alone would not be able to capture. An example of this is when the mother in the story states that she feels isolated. Those words, while important, can be viewed as a vague description of the situation. In contrast, through the drawing we can see her standing alone while a group of women behind her gossip about her circumstances. This allows viewers to actually see the isolation she’s experiencing and perhaps even relate to it.

Illustrations can bring attention to moments that we may miss or intimate situations that may be difficult to describe. A simple example of this is the use of color and detail within the drawings that are included. One of the slides in the story shows the mother covering her face with both of her hands. Viewers could assume that she is feeling frustration, anger, fear, or any other range of emotions. However, from the harsh black background that shifts into a grey and blue tone, viewers understand that she is most likely feeling a deep sense of sadness and distress. The ability to experiment with color also changes the mood of the art and alters how viewers interpret the material.

In addition, there is a separate illustration in which we see a close up version of the mothers face. Rather than relying on color to contribute to the story, the illustration emphasizes certain aspects of her facial expressions in order to create a focus on the pain she experiences. This differs from photos which usually draw focus to specific concepts using the elements of design. While both illustrations and photographs allow the audience to create a visual connection, one of the most significant differences is observed when dealing with tragic representations of real life. This is to say that illustrations make difficult content easier to take in and process. In this story, there is an illustration where the son is visibly ill in his mothers arms, making it an uncomfortable scene to witness. Seeing it as a drawing, still lets the audience visually observe the situation and empathize without experiencing as much discomfort or having the overwhelming feelings that a photograph might otherwise create. Illustrations are able to manipulate the intensity that the audience feels while at the same time getting across an important narrative or concept.
